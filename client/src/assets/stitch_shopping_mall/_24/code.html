The Narrative

"A dialogue between geological permanence and human touch."

Designed by the Curated Studio, the Obsidian Monolith series explores the concept of 'Frozen Gravity'. Each vessel is hand-thrown in a custom-blend stoneware that incorporates volcanic sand from the Etna region.

The firing process takes 72 hours in a reductive kiln atmosphere, resulting in a finish that is neither purely matte nor fully reflective, but possesses a deep, light-absorbing quality unique to this collection.

Weight: 4.2kg. Dimensions: 32cm H x 24cm W.
