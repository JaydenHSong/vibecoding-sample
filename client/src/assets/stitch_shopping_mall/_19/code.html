02. Curation & Artistic Ethics

"The Gallery operates under a mandate of absolute authenticity. Every item presented within our archives has undergone rigorous provenance verification."

Our curation team employs a hybrid methodology of traditional scholarship and digital forensic analysis. We maintain that the digital representation of an artwork is a unique vessel of value, distinct from, yet intimately tied to, its physical counterpart.

Users agree not to misrepresent the origin, state, or intent of any items acquired through the platform. The Gallery reserves the right to de-list or freeze any asset if provenance concerns emerge post-acquisition.
