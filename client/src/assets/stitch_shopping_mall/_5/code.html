How do I track my artwork delivery? expand_more
All curated pieces are shipped via specialized fine art couriers. You can track your shipment through the link provided in your shipping confirmation email, or via the 'Orders' section in your personal profile.
Is international shipping available for all collections? expand_more
We currently ship to over 40 countries. However, certain large-scale sculptures and fragile archive pieces may have regional restrictions due to climate control requirements during transport.
What is your return policy for archive items? expand_more
Archive items are considered final sale due to their historical nature. We provide detailed condition reports and high-resolution visuals prior to purchase to ensure complete satisfaction.

Need personal assistance?

Our curators are available for 1:1 consultations regarding acquisitions, shipping, or technical support.
